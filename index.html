Anomaly

Road Anomaly Detection

Early stage: the dataset has been collected. The planned prototype will use a smartphone mounted in a vehicle to combine accelerometer, gyroscope, speedometer, GPS, and timestamp data to identify possible potholes and estimate their approximate coordinates. The planned detection approach will combine machine learning with rule-based detection and physics-based checks to verify pothole flags, using the Smartphone Sensor Data of Indian Cities for Public Bus dataset from IIT Kharagpur, covering Kolkata, Bhubaneswar, and Durgapur. Preprocessing and model selection are upcoming. The goal is to reduce road survey costs.
