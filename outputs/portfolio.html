Sensor-Based Road Anomaly Detection

Early stage

Dataset collected; preprocessing and model selection are upcoming. The planned prototype uses a vehicle-mounted smartphone’s accelerometer, gyroscope, speedometer, GPS, and timestamps to flag possible potholes and estimate their approximate coordinates. The planned approach combines machine learning, rules, and physics-based verification to help reduce road survey costs. Dataset: Smartphone Sensor Data of Indian Cities for Public Bus, covering Kolkata, Bhubaneswar, and Durgapur.
